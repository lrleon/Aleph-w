# Aleph-w 7.7.4 release notes

Language: English | [Español](7.7.4.es.md)

Aleph-w 7.7.4 is a patch release. It fixes the 20 real defects among
the 72 that the Coverity Scan of 7.7.3 newly detected, and a double
free in `Huffman_Encoder_Engine` that two resource leaks reported by
Scan hid: when memory ran out for a new symbol, the encoder kept a
freed leaf, which it then read and freed again.

It is source compatible with 7.7.3, but the constructors, moves and
swaps of the trees and the default constructor of the buckets of the
open hash tables are no longer `noexcept` when the types of the user may
throw, nor the sifts of `DynArrayHeap` when its comparison may throw:
what they throw there now reaches the caller, where it ended the program
(see upgrade note 2), and a `DynArrayHeap` is then left out of order
(see the [known limits](#known-limits)). As with every release,
recompile everything against the new headers
([`VERSIONING.md`](../../VERSIONING.md#abi-compatibility)).

## Upgrade notes

1. `find_package(Aleph 7 ...)` accepts 7.7.4.
2. The constructors, moves and swaps of the thirteen trees, and the
   default constructor of the buckets of `OLhashTable` and
   `ODhashTable`, are `noexcept` only when the types of the user do not
   throw. With a key whose default construction may throw, or a
   comparison whose copy, move or swap may throw, they let that
   exception reach the caller, where 7.7.3 called `std::terminate()`.
   `sift_up()` and `sift_down()` of `DynArrayHeap` are `noexcept` only
   when the comparison does not throw, and then an exception of a move
   of the items still ends the program. With types that do not throw,
   all of them stay `noexcept`, except two constructors of
   `Gen_Rand_Tree`, which allocate its generator: the default one, no
   longer `noexcept`, and the move constructor, which was not. The
   default constructor of the nodes follows that of the key. Code that
   asks with `noexcept(...)` or with the traits of `<type_traits>` gets
   the new answers. The splay trees keep their operations `noexcept`, as
   `tpl_splay_tree.H` documents.
3. The value of a `Synchronized` or an `RwSynchronized` built by default
   is `T()`: zero for an arithmetic type or a pointer, where it was
   indeterminate.

## Fixes

- **trees, hash, heap** (#210): the constructors of ten trees build the
  key of their header nodes and copy the comparison, the default
  constructor of the buckets of `OLhashTable` and `ODhashTable` builds
  their key, and the sifts of `DynArrayHeap` call the comparison. All
  were `noexcept` without a condition, so that an exception there ended
  the program, where 7.7.2 made the other operations of the trees let
  it reach the caller (17 CIDs, upgrade note 2). The swaps and the move
  assignments now exchange the comparison first, so that, if that
  throws, the nodes stay where they were.
- **concurrency** (#210): `Synchronized` and `RwSynchronized`
  default-initialized their value, though their documentation says that
  they build it (2 CIDs, upgrade note 3).
- **csv** (#210): the count of the bytes that `csv_skip_bom()` gives
  back wrapped around past zero after its loop. Nothing read it again,
  and the loop now stops at zero (1 CID).
- **huffman** (#212): when the map of the symbols ran out of memory for
  a new symbol, in `set_freq()`, `read_input()` or
  `set_end_of_stream()`, the heap of the encoder kept the node of the
  symbol, whose leaf had been freed: the encoder then read that leaf
  and freed it a second time. The node now enters the map first, the
  only step that can throw, and the heap after it, so that the
  exception leaves the encoder as it was. `set_end_of_stream()` copies
  the symbol before it enters: without memory for the copy, the encoder
  kept the symbol, but not as its end, and a second call could not set
  it.
- **graph, heap, trees** (#212): `GraphCommon::insert_arc()`,
  `Fibonacci_Heap::insert()` and `emplace()`,
  `DynIntervalTree::insert()` and `insert_dup()`, and `new_tree_node()`
  of `generate_df_tree.H` give their new node to their container with
  a local guard, which frees it only if the insertion throws or, in the
  interval tree, if the interval was there already, instead of
  returning `unique_ptr::release()`. Coverity took that result for
  memory that the caller must free, and reported a leak wherever a
  caller ignored the node that it got back: 42 resource leaks of the
  library, and those of the code of the users of Aleph who analyze it
  with Coverity. It was not a defect, and the behavior is the same.

## Performance

- **trees, hash, heap, concurrency** (#210): no cost measured with keys
  and comparisons that do not throw. GCC 14 and Clang 18, at `-O2`, run
  the same count of user-space instructions to build and destroy
  2 × 10^6 `Avl_Tree`, `Rb_Tree` and `TdRbTree`, to insert and remove
  2 × 10^5 keys in `Avl_Tree`, `Rb_Tree` and `Treap`, for 5 × 10^5
  insertions and `getMin()` in a `DynArrayHeap<int>`, and to build
  2 × 10^6 `Synchronized<long>`.
- **graph, heap** (#212): cachegrind, at `-O2` with 10^5 operations,
  counts the same instructions per operation before and after: 1116.79
  with GCC 14 and 1103.80 with Clang 18 for an arc of `List_Graph`,
  761.11 and 757.11 for an arc of `List_Digraph`, and 1083.14 and
  1076.14 for the three insertions of `Fibonacci_Heap` (`insert()` of a
  copy and of a move, and `emplace()`).

## Tests

- `noexcept_construction_test` checks, in 67 cases on the thirteen
  trees, the buckets and the sifts, that the operations are `noexcept`
  with types that do not throw, and not with a key whose default
  construction may throw or a comparison whose copy, move or swap may
  throw, and that what those types throw reaches the caller. On 7.7.3
  its first failure ends the program.
- `ConcurrencyUtils.DefaultValueIsValueInitialized` builds both
  wrappers over a buffer with garbage and reads zero.
- `huffman_allocation_test` makes each allocation of `set_freq()` and
  of `set_end_of_stream()` fail in turn, and checks that the encoder
  then takes the symbol and that its tree has two leaves. Without the
  fix, its first case ends the program with a heap-use-after-free under
  ASan.

## Known limits

- `DynIntervalTree::insert()` and `insert_dup()` still free a node that
  they left linked when the comparison throws after the link, in the
  update of the maximum or in a rotation, so that the tree keeps a freed
  node. 7.7.3 did the same; the fix goes to a later release, with its
  test.
- When the comparison throws in `insert()`, `insert_direct()`, `put()`
  or `append()` of a `DynArrayHeap`, or in `getMin()`, `get()` or
  `getMax()`, the exception reaches the caller, but the heap is left
  out of order: the insertions already stored and counted the item, and
  the extractions already took out the first item of the heap, the
  minimum with the default comparison, which is lost. The same happens
  when a move of the items throws there, if the comparison may throw.
  Do not use the heap after such an exception; 7.7.3 ended the program
  instead.
