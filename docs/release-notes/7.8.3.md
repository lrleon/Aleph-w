# Aleph-w 7.8.3 release notes

Language: English | [Español](7.8.3.es.md)

Aleph-w 7.8.3 is a patch release. It fixes three defects that the work
of 7.8.2 found: the copy of a raw linear hash table shared the buckets
of the other table; the open and the linear hash tables did not compile
their swaps and some of their moves with a comparator that can be copied
and not moved; and eighteen trees and binary heaps ended the program
when built by default with a comparison whose default construction
throws.

It is source compatible with 7.8.2, but a raw `LinearHashTable` can no
longer be copied (see upgrade note 2). As with every release, recompile
everything against the new headers
([`VERSIONING.md`](../../VERSIONING.md#abi-compatibility)).

## Upgrade notes

1. `find_package(Aleph 7 ...)` accepts 7.8.3.
2. A raw `LinearHashTable` or `LinearHashTableVtl` cannot be copied: the
   copy that the compiler wrote shared the buckets of the other table,
   and destroying one of the two corrupted the memory of the other, so
   that code that copied one no longer compiles. Nor can it be moved,
   since its move was that same copy: `swap()` exchanges two tables in
   constant time. `DynSetLinHash` and `DynMapLinHash`, which copy their
   keys, copy and move as before.
3. The default construction of `BinTree`, `Avl_Tree`, `Avl_Tree_Rk`,
   `Rb_Tree`, `Rb_Tree_Rk` and `Splay_Tree`, of their `Vtl`, of the
   `Vtl` of `HtdRbTree` and `HtdRbTreeRk`, and of `BinHeap` and
   `BinHeapVtl`, is `noexcept` only when the default construction of the
   comparison and its copy from a comparison that is not const are; so
   is the construction of the trees from a comparison. With
   `Aleph::less` and any comparison that cannot throw, they stay
   `noexcept`. They were `noexcept` whatever the comparison threw.

## Fixes

- **hash** (#225): the copy of a `LinearHashTable` itself, which the
  compiler generated, copied the links of the lists, which then pointed
  to the buckets of the other table: a copy of a table of 10 keys said
  that it had 10, and destroying one of the two tables freed the buckets
  that the other one still held. The copy and the copy assignment of
  `GenLinearHashTable` are deleted, as those of `GenLhashTable` (upgrade
  note 2).
- **hash** (#225): `ODhashTable`, `OLhashTable` and `GenLinearHashTable`
  swapped their comparators with `std::swap()`, which asks for a type
  that can be moved: with a comparator that can be copied and not moved,
  which `EqualityComparator` admits, the move assignment and `swap()` of
  `ODhashTable` and `OLhashTable`, `swap()` of the linear table, and the
  moves and `swap()` of `DynSetLinHash` did not compile. They swap such
  a comparator through its copies, as `GenLhashTable` does since 7.8.2;
  with one that can be moved, the code is the one of 7.8.2.
- **tree** (#225): the trees above and the binary heaps inherit their
  constructors, which took the comparison as a default argument, and GCC
  and Clang do not count a default argument in the `noexcept` of an
  implicit default constructor: built by default with a comparison whose
  default construction throws, they ended the program. And the
  constructor that takes the comparison copies it from one that is not
  const, while its `noexcept` asked for the copy from a const one, so
  that a comparison whose copy from `Compare &` throws ended the program
  in sixteen trees. The nine bases have a default constructor of their
  own, which counts the construction and the copy of the comparison, and
  the one that takes the comparison asks for the copy that it makes
  (upgrade note 3).

## Tests

- `Tests/noexcept_construction_test.cc` goes from 176 tests to 205: each
  of the fourteen trees, and those whose nodes have a virtual
  destructor, built by default with a comparison whose default
  construction throws and from a temporary comparison whose copy from
  one that is not const throws; what they throw reaches the caller, and
  their `noexcept` says so. `Tests/binheap.cc` does the same with the
  binary heaps.
- `Tests/hash-it.cc`, the test of the pattern of the comparator that
  cannot be moved, swaps, moves and move assigns `ODhashTable`,
  `OLhashTable` and `DynSetLinHash` with it, and checks that their
  comparators are swapped. `Tests/lin-hash.cc` checks that a linear
  table cannot be copied and that `DynSetLinHash` copies its keys.
- With any of the 12 headers whose code changes taken back to 7.8.2, a
  test fails; and each of 35 mutations of the fixes, one at a time,
  makes a test fail or not compile.
- Every test program of `Tests/` runs under ASan and UBSan with GCC 14:
  467 programs, with 16 785 tests in the 464 of GoogleTest. The four
  programs that change run with the UBSan of Clang 18, and a sample of
  35 programs compiles with GCC 11 and with Clang 14 and libc++, the
  compilers of ubuntu-22.04 in the CI, and adds no kind of warning to
  those of 7.8.2 with GCC 14 and Clang 18.

## Known limits

- On `Treap`, `Treap_Rk` and `Rand_Tree`, the tree that the move of a
  `DynSetTree` builds for the set that it moves from makes its generator
  of random numbers, a block of `malloc()` of 2528 bytes, with any key.
  The `noexcept` of the move does not count it: without memory for it,
  the program ends. And the move of a `DynSetLinHash` or a
  `DynMapLinHash` builds a linear table, whose `DynArray` makes its
  directory with `malloc()`, and a default value that holds a key built
  by default. Both are left for 7.9.0, with a move of a tree that builds
  nothing, which will make the move of a set `noexcept` with every key
  again.
- The move constructor of a hash table copies its hash function, so
  that the table that it moves from keeps one that goes with its
  comparator. A pointer to a function, and a function object that fits
  in the buffer of a `std::function`, are copied without allocating;
  with one that does not fit, the move of a `LhashTable`, a
  `DynSetLhash`, a `DynSetHash`, a `DynMapHash` or an `ODhashTable`
  allocates once, in a constructor that is `noexcept`. Their move
  assignments copy nothing but a comparator that cannot be moved.
- MSVC moves the arguments of a constructor that a class inherits: with
  a comparator that can be copied and not moved, as one whose move
  constructor is deleted, it cannot build a `LhashTable`, a
  `LhashTableVtl`, a `DynLhashTable`, a `DynSetLhash` or a `DynMapHash`,
  however they are built, since they are built through the constructors
  that `LhashTable` and `LhashTableVtl` inherit. GCC, Clang and clang-cl
  build them.
- `Static_Event_Table` and `Simplex` leave to `new[]` a count of the
  caller whose bytes may not fit in a `size_t`, as `MemArray` did, and
  the hash tables do the same with their buckets, after they round
  their length up to a prime.
- In `ArrayHeap`, with a comparison that cannot throw, a move
  assignment of an item that throws in `remove()` leaves the entry of
  the removed item as that assignment left it. With a comparison that
  may throw, a removed item that cannot be emptied without a throw
  stays in its entry, with what it holds, and so does the last item of
  the array with any comparison, until another item takes the entry or
  the array is destroyed.
- A move of the items of a `DynArrayHeap` that throws still leaves the
  heap out of order. When the key of `Fibonacci_Heap::decrease_key()`
  throws in its assignment, the node keeps what the assignment left,
  and may be out of order.
- In the interval tree, if the last assignment of a maximum throws, a
  move assignment that throws or the copy assignment of an endpoint
  that has no other, the maxima of some nodes may be left changed,
  though no node moves: the queries may then miss intervals, and the
  tree must be destroyed or built again.
- `heapsort()` and `faster_heapsort()` still form a pointer to one item
  before the array when they sort two items or more.
- Some `noexcept` of the library, in the interval tree and in the graphs
  among others, look at the implicit conversion of the result of a
  comparison to `bool`, where the code makes the one of a condition: for
  a result whose two conversions differ, they may promise what the call
  does not keep.
- The iterators of the library keep their positions in a `long`, of 32
  bits on 64-bit Windows: on a container of 2^31 items or more, which
  `DynArray` admits, they would give a negative number of items. They
  will change together, in a later release.
