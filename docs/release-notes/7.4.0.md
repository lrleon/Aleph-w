# Aleph-w 7.4.0 release notes

Language: English | [Español](7.4.0.es.md)

Aleph-w 7.4.0 is a minor release that replaces GSL. The randomized
structures, the random generators and `LFit` no longer call GSL: their
random numbers come from `Aleph::Gsl_Compatible_Rng`, of the new header
`ah-random.H`, which gives, for each seed, the numbers of
`gsl_rng_mt19937`, and `LFit` makes the computations of GSL itself. A
seed makes the same treaps, skip lists, graphs, networks, trees, cuts,
samples and tables as with GSL, with fewer instructions. The new CMake
option `ALEPH_WITH_GSL`, ON by default until 8.0.0, keeps linking GSL as
before; with OFF, Aleph neither needs nor links GSL, and the programs
that link Aleph get no GPL library from it. 7.4.0 also fixes the errors
that `LFit` reports, the moves of `Treap` and `Treap_Rk` and three
defects of the generators, and deprecates `gsl_rng_object()`,
`Random_Set::get_rng()`, `ran_array.h` and `aleph-exceptions.H`.

It is source compatible with 7.3.6, but for the changes listed in the
[Upgrade notes](#upgrade-notes). As with every release, recompile
everything against the new headers
([`VERSIONING.md`](../../VERSIONING.md#abi-compatibility)).

## Upgrade notes

1. `find_package(Aleph 7 ...)` accepts 7.4.0.
2. By default, `ALEPH_WITH_GSL` is ON: Aleph still links GSL and passes
   it to the programs that link `Aleph::Aleph`, and the deprecated
   accessors below work as before. To build without GSL, which then
   need not be installed:

   ```bash
   cmake -S . -B build -DALEPH_WITH_GSL=OFF
   ```

   Aleph then defines `ALEPH_WITH_GSL=0` for its consumers, leaves out
   the deprecated `gsl_rng_object()` and `get_rng()`, and its `.deb` and
   `.rpm` do not depend on GSL. A program that uses GSL itself, and took
   it from `Aleph::Aleph`, links it itself then; `find_package(Aleph)`
   sets `Aleph_WITH_GSL` to what the package was built with:

   ```cmake
   find_package(Aleph 7.4 REQUIRED)
   target_link_libraries(app PRIVATE Aleph::Aleph)
   if(NOT Aleph_WITH_GSL)
     find_package(GSL REQUIRED)
     target_link_libraries(app PRIVATE GSL::gsl)
   endif()
   ```

   OFF becomes the default in 8.0.0.
3. Of the headers of GSL, Aleph includes only `<gsl/gsl_rng.h>`, from
   `ah-random.H` and while `ALEPH_WITH_GSL` is 1. In 7.3.6, 13 headers
   included it, `random_net.H` included `<gsl/gsl_randist.h>` too, and
   `lfit.H` `<gsl/gsl_fit.h>` and `<gsl/gsl_statistics_double.h>`: a
   program that uses GSL includes the headers that it needs.
4. `gsl_rng_object()` of `Treap`, `Rand_Tree`, `SkipList`, `DynSkipList`
   and `Interval_Tree`, and `get_rng()` of `Random_Set`, are deprecated,
   and calling them gives a compiler warning. `rng()` gives the
   generator of the structure, whose members draw what the functions of
   GSL drew from its `gsl_rng` ([New](#new)):

   ```cpp
   // 7.3.6
   gsl_rng * r = treap.gsl_rng_object();
   unsigned long k = gsl_rng_uniform_int(r, 100);

   // 7.4.0
   unsigned long k = treap.rng().uniform_int(100);
   ```

   The deprecated accessors may throw `std::bad_alloc` the first time,
   when they make their `gsl_rng`, and are no longer `noexcept`. Their
   `gsl_rng` acts on the generator of the structure, as before, but a
   file that `gsl_rng_fwrite()` writes from it holds the 2500 bytes of
   the state of a `Gsl_Compatible_Rng`, in the byte order of the
   machine, and is not one of a `gsl_rng_mt19937`.
5. `LFit` reports other errors. `mse` is `sumsq / n`, the mean squared
   error, and `sigma` the root of `sumsq / (n - 2)`, the standard
   deviation of the errors that the covariances estimate, NaN for two
   points. 7.3.6 took the errors from `x - y` instead of the residuals:
   for `y = 2x + 1` on `x = 1, ..., 5` it gave an `mse` of 4.243 and a
   `sigma` of 1.414, where both are 0 now; with errors of ±0.1 on that
   line, 4.263 and 1.418, where they are 0.0096 and 0.126. `c`, `m`,
   the covariances, `sumsq` and `r2` do not change. `LFit` of empty
   arrays throws `SizeMismatch`, where it read out of them.
6. `Treap`, `Treap_Rk` and `RandTree` cannot be copied: a copy compiled,
   and both freed the same `gsl_rng`. A move of a treap takes its nodes
   and its generator, and leaves the moved one without them: it can
   then only be swapped or destroyed.
7. `rand_gen` (`euclidian-graph-common.H`) is a `Gsl_Compatible_Rng *`,
   and the protected `r` of `Random_Graph_Base` a
   `Gsl_Compatible_Rng_Owner`: code that used them as a `gsl_rng *`
   calls their members.
8. `Reservoir_Sampler` throws `std::bad_alloc`, instead of
   `std::runtime_error`, when there is no memory for its generator, and
   `std::runtime_error` for the element after 2^32 - 1 ones, where GSL
   ended the program.
9. `tpl_treapRk.H` no longer includes `ran_array.h`: a program that
   called `ran_start()` or `ran_arr_next()` through it includes
   `ran_array.h` itself, which is deprecated ([Deprecated](#deprecated)).
10. A program that names a class of `aleph-exceptions.H`, as
    `SizeMismatch`, or uses `DEFINE_ALEPH_EXCEPTION` or `ALEPHTHROW`,
    warns of their deprecation ([Deprecated](#deprecated)); one that
    only includes `lfit.H`, or catches the errors of `LFit` as
    `std::runtime_error` or `std::exception`, does not.

## New

- **`Aleph::Gsl_Compatible_Rng` (`ah-random.H`).** A Mersenne Twister
  seeded as GSL seeds `gsl_rng_mt19937` (0 is 4357, and else the low 32
  bits count), whose draws follow the algorithms of GSL:

  | Member | GSL |
  |---|---|
  | `Gsl_Compatible_Rng()`, `seed(s)` | `gsl_rng_alloc(gsl_rng_mt19937)`, `gsl_rng_set(r, s)` |
  | `operator()()`, `get()` | `gsl_rng_get(r)` |
  | `uniform_int(n)` | `gsl_rng_uniform_int(r, n)` |
  | `uniform()`, `uniform_pos()` | `gsl_rng_uniform(r)`, `gsl_rng_uniform_pos(r)` |
  | `flat(a, b)`, `gaussian(sigma)`, `exponential(mu)` | `gsl_ran_flat()`, `gsl_ran_gaussian()`, `gsl_ran_exponential()` |

  Its numbers are those of `std::mt19937`, from inline code, and its
  state takes 2500 bytes. It satisfies
  `std::uniform_random_bit_generator`, so the distributions and the
  algorithms of the standard library take it. `uniform_int(n)` throws
  `std::domain_error` for `n` = 0 or above 2^32 - 1, where GSL calls its
  error handler, which ends the program by default. `flat()` and
  `gaussian()` round each product, so that a fused multiply-add of the
  compiler changes no draw: they give the bits of GSL where GSL rounds
  them too, as on x86-64. `gaussian()` and `exponential()` take the
  logarithm of the C library, so they may differ from GSL in the last
  bits where GSL takes that of another one, as on Windows; and
  `gaussian()` where GSL fuses `x * x + y * y`, as the GSL of the
  distributions for arm64 does.
- **`rng()`** gives the generator of `Treap`, `Treap_Rk`, `Rand_Tree`,
  `SkipList`, `DynSkipList`, `Random_Set` and `Interval_Tree`. They keep
  it through a `Gsl_Compatible_Rng_Owner`, a pointer to memory of
  `malloc()`, as they kept their `gsl_rng`, so they keep their size.
- **`ALEPH_WITH_GSL`**, an option of CMake ([Upgrade
  notes](#upgrade-notes)); `find_package(Aleph)` sets `Aleph_WITH_GSL`,
  and `Aleph::Aleph` has the property `ALEPH_WITH_GSL`. The recipes
  of Conan, vcpkg and Spack take GSL as an option, on by default until
  8.0.0, as `ALEPH_WITH_GSL`, and the Python wheels are built without
  it.

## Deprecated

- `gsl_rng_object()` of `Treap`, `Rand_Tree`, `SkipList`, `DynSkipList`
  and `Interval_Tree`, and `Random_Set::get_rng()`. Use `rng()` instead.
  They exist while `ALEPH_WITH_GSL` is 1, and go in 8.0.0, as the
  [deprecation procedure](../../VERSIONING.md#deprecation-procedure)
  provides.
- `ran_array.h` (`ran_start()`, `ran_arr_next()`, `ran_arr_ptr` and
  `ran_arr_cycle()`), Knuth's generator, which nothing of Aleph uses. Use
  the engines of `<random>` instead. It goes in 8.0.0 with
  `ran_array.c`: Knuth's notice puts his code in the public domain only
  as long as it is not changed, and this copy is changed.
- `aleph-exceptions.H`: `AlephException`, its 19 exceptions
  (`SizeMismatch`, `InvalidValue`, `OutOfRange` and the others),
  `DEFINE_ALEPH_EXCEPTION` and `ALEPHTHROW`. Use the macros of
  `ah-errors.H` instead, which throw the exceptions of the standard
  library. It goes in 8.0.0. Until then, `LFit` throws `SizeMismatch`,
  a `std::runtime_error`; from 8.0.0 it throws `std::invalid_argument`,
  as the macros of `ah-errors.H` do: catching `std::exception` catches
  both.

## Fixes

- **A moved treap kept the head of the other one.** `Treap` and
  `Treap_Rk` keep their root in a link of their head, and had no
  constructor to move: a treap returned from one of two variables
  pointed to the head of that variable, which no longer existed, and
  both freed the same `gsl_rng`. A move takes the nodes and the
  generator now, and is `noexcept` when the move of the comparison
  criteria is.
- **A copy of `RandTree` compiled,** and both freed the same `gsl_rng`.
- **`gen_random_euclidian_graph()` left `rand_gen` set when the random
  graph threw,** to a `gsl_rng` that it never freed.
- **`Reservoir_Sampler::update()` took the element after 2^32 - 1
  ones,** whose draw among 2^32 positions made GSL end the program: it
  throws `std::runtime_error`, as for the following ones.
- **`LFit` took its errors from `x - y`,** and read out of empty arrays
  ([Upgrade notes](#upgrade-notes)).

## Tests

- `ah_random_test` (new): the draws of `Gsl_Compatible_Rng`, bit by bit
  against GSL, interleaved, for 13 seeds, and without GSL by their
  hashes; its numbers against `std::mt19937`; its errors, ranges,
  moments and copies.
- `random_structures_test` (new): for 6 seeds, the trees with their
  priorities, the levels of the skip lists, the order of a random queue
  and the next number of each generator are those that the structures
  made with GSL; the deprecated `gsl_rng`; moves and swaps.
- `random_generators_test` (new): for 6 seeds, what the graphs,
  digraphs, networks, trees, cuts of Karger, seeds of a Bloom filter,
  samples of a reservoir, Euclidean graphs and the table of `jsw_hash()`
  make is what they made with GSL.
- `lfit_test`: the fit, the covariances, `sumsq`, R² and the predictions
  of 810 data sets, bit by bit against GSL, and the errors from the
  residuals.
- `ran_array_test` (new): the number of Knuth's check.
- `aleph_exceptions_test` (new): each exception of `aleph-exceptions.H`
  and `DEFINE_ALEPH_EXCEPTION`, as before; and a check that compiles
  programs, that the deprecation warns where a program uses them, and
  not where it includes `lfit.H` or uses `LFit`.
- The new job `without-gsl` of the CI builds and tests everything where
  GSL is not installed: the 10 cases that compare with GSL report
  `SKIPPED`. On Windows, MSVC builds with GSL, whose comparisons pass
  there, and `clang-cl` without it.

## Performance

own, minus the instructions of an empty one, with `time()` fixed, and
both versions give the same sums; each figure is the change in the
instructions:
