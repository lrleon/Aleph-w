# Aleph-w 7.3.4 release notes

Language: English | [Español](7.3.4.es.md)

Aleph-w 7.3.4 is a patch release of the lists, the components of a graph
and the flows. The removes of `DynList`, `DynListStack` and
`DynListQueue` keep the first item when its move throws, where they lost
it or ended the program; `Unconnected_Components` and
`inconnected_components()` throw on a digraph, where they gave wrong
components; `map_in_arcs()` and `map_out_arcs()` with `T` given take an
actual `std::function`, with which they were ambiguous; and the random
preflow takes a seed, so that its runs can be repeated.

It is source compatible with 7.3.3, but for the changes listed in the
[Upgrade notes](#upgrade-notes). As with every release, recompile
everything against the new headers
([`VERSIONING.md`](../../VERSIONING.md#abi-compatibility)).

## Upgrade notes

1. `find_package(Aleph 7 ...)` accepts 7.3.4.
2. `Unconnected_Components` and `inconnected_components()` throw
   `domain_error` on a digraph, as `test_connectivity()` does: they follow
   the arcs that leave each node, which do not give the components of a
   digraph (see Fixes). The strongly connected components of a digraph
   are those of `Tarjan_Connected_Components`.
3. `DynList::remove_ne()` and `remove_first_ne()` are `noexcept` only when
   the move of the item is; they were `noexcept` whatever it was.
4. A floating flow of `tpl_net.H` does not compile with `-ffast-math` or
   `/fp:fast`: its sums keep what their rounding loses, which needs strict
   floating arithmetic.

## New

- `random_preflow_maximum_flow(net, seed)`, whose random order of the
  active nodes takes a seed, so that its pushes can be repeated; and
  `generic_preflow_vertex_push_maximum_flow()` takes the queue of the
  active nodes, by default a new one.

## Fixes

- `DynList`, `DynListStack` and `DynListQueue` unlinked the node of the
  first item before they moved the item out: a move that threw lost the
  item and its node, and `remove_ne()` ended the program; the stack and
  the queue then counted one item more than they held. They move it
  before its node leaves, once when the move can throw, so that if it
  throws they keep the item and their size.
- `Unconnected_Components` and `inconnected_components()` on a digraph
  gave wrong components, and the copy of an arc toward a component built
  before pointed to a node of another subgraph (Upgrade notes 2).
- `map_in_arcs<GT, T>(p, op)` and `map_out_arcs<GT, T>(p, op)` were
  ambiguous when `op` was an actual `std::function`.
- GCC 12 and on warned that `free_detached_tree()` of `tpl_tree_node.H`
  stores the address of a local list in a node, which the list leaves
  before the end, in a build with optimizations; the warning is off
  around the function.

## Tests

- The tests of `DynList`, `DynListStack` and `DynListQueue` are built, as
  those of `DynDlist`, without the elisions that the language does not
  require (`-fno-elide-constructors`), with GCC and Clang, so that they
  see a move that a compiler may not elide.
- A compile check, `NetFlows.FloatingFlowsNeedStrictArithmetic`, compiles
  a floating preflow with `-ffast-math`, which must fail.
- The eight files of the contract matrix share `for_each_filter()` and its
  helpers in `Tests/graph_contract_matrix.H`; on a digraph, the matrix
  expects `domain_error` from every operation of `Unconnected_Components`.

## Performance

- For a type whose move cannot throw, the removes of `DynList`,
  `DynListStack` and `DynListQueue` give the same code as in 7.3.3, with
  GCC 14 and Clang 22 at `-O2`.
- With integer flows, the FIFO and heap preflows run the same
  instructions as in 7.3.3 or fewer, counted with cachegrind at `-O2`: up
  to 3.5 % fewer with GCC 14 (300 nodes, about 3300 arcs).
