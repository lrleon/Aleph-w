# Aleph-w 7.8.5 release notes

Language: English | [Español](7.8.5.es.md)

Aleph-w 7.8.5 is a patch release. It fixes the merge of the two
quotient filters, which in the compact one dropped without a word the
items that did not fit, so that the filter then denied items that it
had taken; the filters that `from_capacity()` built for a count of
items and a rate that no filter has, or for a rate just under a power
of two; and the default operation of `maps()`, which converted each
item three times and copied it twice. And it closes four of the known
limits of 7.8.4: the sizes that `Static_Event_Table`,
`Simplex` and the hash tables cannot hold, the conversion of the result
of a comparison or of a filter where it is a condition, the pointer
that the heapsorts formed before the array, and what `ArrayHeap` keeps
when an operation of an item throws.

It is source compatible with 7.8.4. As with every release, recompile
everything against the new headers
([`VERSIONING.md`](../../VERSIONING.md#abi-compatibility)).

## Upgrade notes

1. `find_package(Aleph 7 ...)` accepts 7.8.5.
2. `merge()` of `Quotient_Filter` and of `Compact_Quotient_Filter` takes
   every item of the other filter, or throws `std::overflow_error` and
   leaves the filter as it was. In every earlier version since 3.1.1,
   `Compact_Quotient_Filter::merge()` returned without the items that
   did not fit, and `Quotient_Filter::merge()` threw with some of them
   in. A caller that merges filters whose items do not fit gets the
   exception now.
3. `from_capacity()` and `estimate()` of the two filters throw
   `std::domain_error` for a count of items and a rate of false
   positives that no filter has: a rate that asks for more than 60
   remainder bits, a count that asks for more than 2^32 slots, or the
   two together for more than the 64 bits of the hash. They gave a
   filter with fewer slots than items, or with another rate. For every
   other pair they give the same filter as before, but for a rate just
   under a power of two, as 0.2499…, which gets one remainder bit more:
   they rounded it to the power, and the filter had a rate above the
   one asked.
4. `maps<R>()` without an operation, of a container made of the mixins
   of `ah-dry-mixin.H`, gives each item converted to `R` once: its
   values change only where the way back from `R` to the type of the
   items lost something. `maps()` without an operation and without `R`
   copies each item once, and moves none. And
   `Dft_Map_Op::operator()` and `Dft_Fold_Op::operator()` are
   `noexcept` only for the types whose copy, conversion or default
   construction is, and `std::invocable` is false for them with types
   that they cannot build, where it was true and the call did not
   compile.
5. The constructors of `Static_Event_Table`, `Simplex` and the hash
   tables, and `resize()` of the tables, throw `std::overflow_error` for
   a count whose bytes do not fit in a `size_t`, where they threw
   `std::bad_array_new_length` or `std::bad_alloc`, took seconds to do
   so, or ended the program under a sanitizer. `Simplex(n)` refuses too
   the largest `n` whose bytes fit, since a restriction has one
   coefficient more. `Cuckoo_Filter`, `Compact_Cuckoo_Filter` and
   `Bloom_Filter::estimate()` throw it too, for a count of items whose
   buckets or bits a `size_t` does not count, where a cuckoo filter
   could be built with one bucket. `Bloom_Filter::estimate()`, and the
   constructor that takes the items and the probability, throw
   `std::invalid_argument` for a probability that is not a number, where
   they went on with 2^63 bits.
6. The functions that take what a comparison or a filter returns as a
   condition are `noexcept` only when the conversion to `bool` that a
   condition makes of it is: `overlaps()`, `contains()` and `is_valid()`
   of `Interval`, `Interval_Less`, `search()` of the trees of intervals,
   `search_arc()`, `search_directed_arc()`, `next_ne()` and `prev_ne()`
   of the iterators that take a filter, the filter of the arcs that
   Kruskal paints, `find_ptr()`, `find_index()` and `find_item()` of the
   containers, `selection_sort()`, `insertion_sort()`, `select_pivot()`
   and `partition()`, `Negate_Compare`, `Inversed_Compare`,
   `sort_nodes()` and `sort_arcs()` of the graphs, the functors that
   compare by default (`Aleph::less`, `greater`, `less_equal`,
   `greater_equal`, `equal_to`, `not_equal_to`, `logical_and`,
   `logical_or` and `logical_not`) and `unary_negate`, the equality of
   `Interval`, `search()`, `contains()` and `has()` of `DynListStack`
   and `search()` of `DynListQueue`, `find_node()` and `find_arc()` of
   the graphs, `next_ne()`, `reset_first()` and `reset_last()` of the
   two `Digraph_Iterator`, and `end()` of the one of `tpl_graph.H`.
   `In_Iterator` on a digraph and the filter of Kruskal look for it at
   the call of their filter with an arc that has a name, which is the
   one that they make. And `traverse()` of `DynList`, `ArrayHeap`,
   `DynArrayHeap`, `MemArray`, `ArrayStack`, `FixedStack`, `ArrayQueue`,
   `FixedQueue`, `DynArray` and `DynSkipList` converts what its
   operation returns, and no longer calls the negation of a result that
   defines one. With a result that is a `bool`, nothing changes.

## Fixes

- **filter** (#233): `merge()` of `Compact_Quotient_Filter` ignored what
  the insertion of each item said: when the items of the two filters did
  not fit, those that found no slot were dropped, and `merge()`
  returned. The filter then denied items that it had been given, where
  its header promises no false negatives: two filters of 16 slots with
  12 different items each gave one that denied 8 of the 12 that it took,
  and of 2400 merges of filters with random items, 845 dropped items.
  `merge()` of `Quotient_Filter` threw there with some of the items of
  the other filter in. Both count now the items of the other filter that
  this one does not hold, when the two sizes say that they may not fit,
  before any of them enters, and throw if they are more than the free
  slots (upgrade note 2). A merge that counts runs from 40 to 56 % more
  instructions; the others, the same.
- **filter** (#233): a full filter refused an item that it held.
  `insert()` of `Compact_Quotient_Filter` and `merge()` of
  `Quotient_Filter` threw `std::overflow_error` for it, since they
  looked at the room before they looked for the item. The insertion
  looks for the item first now, in both filters.
- **filter** (#233): `from_capacity()` and `estimate()` of the two
  filters took the quotient bits from the count of items and the
  remainder bits from the rate, and when the two were more than the 64
  bits of the hash they took quotient bits away: 2^24 slots for 10^9
  items at a rate of 10^-12. `Quotient_Filter::from_capacity()` and the
  two `estimate()` converted the bits to `uint8_t`, which is undefined
  past 255: a rate of 10^-80 gave a filter with a rate of 2^-10. And a
  rate that is not a number gave one remainder bit. They throw now when
  no filter has the two (upgrade note 3).
- **filter** (#233): `from_capacity()` and `estimate()` took the
  remainder bits from the logarithm of the rate, which a `double`
  rounds: a rate just under a power of two got the bits of the power,
  one fewer than it asks for, and a filter of a rate above the one
  asked. Of the 59 rates just under each power from 2^-1 to 2^-59, 58
  did, and the rate just under 2^-60, which no filter has, got the
  filter of 2^-60. They count the bits now in the exponent of the rate,
  which is exact (upgrade note 3).
- **functional** (#233): `maps<R>()` of the mixins of `ah-dry-mixin.H`,
  called without an operation, gave `Dft_Map_Op` its two types
  exchanged: an item went to `R`, back to its own type and to `R` again.
  `maps<double>()` of the largest 64-bit integer gave -2^63 on x86-64,
  and it did not compile for an `R` from which the type of the items
  cannot be built. And `Dft_Map_Op::operator()` and
  `Dft_Fold_Op::operator()` were `noexcept` while they copied an item or
  build an accumulator: with an item whose copy throws, `maps()` ended
  the program (upgrade note 4).
- **functional** (#233): `maps()` without an operation and without `R`,
  the copy of the items, took each one twice: the default operation
  built a copy, and the container moved it, or copied it again when the
  item has no move. `maps()` copies each item into its result itself
  now, between items of one type, and does not call the default
  operation there; an item whose move is deleted is copied so too
  (upgrade note 4).
- **hash** (#235): `ODhashTable`, `OLhashTable` and `GenLhashTable`,
  with the sets and the maps made of them, left to `new[]` a count of
  buckets of the caller whose bytes do not fit in a `size_t`, after
  `Primes::next_prime()`, which past its table of primes searches by
  trial division: 6 s for 2^61 buckets. They refuse it before both now,
  in their constructors and in `resize()`, which in the table of lists
  checks the size that it is given before it grows from it (upgrade note
  5).
- **filter** (#235): `Cuckoo_Filter` and `Compact_Cuckoo_Filter`
  computed their buckets as a `double` and converted it to `size_t`,
  which is undefined when they are more than a `size_t` counts: with GCC
  14, a filter of one entry per bucket for 2^64 - 1 items was built with
  one bucket. `Bloom_Filter::estimate()` converted its bits in the same
  way. The three refuse such a count now (upgrade note 5).
- **filter** (#235): `Bloom_Filter::estimate()` took a probability that
  is not a number, which is not in (0, 1): its test of the range
  compares it, and every comparison of such a number is false. Its bits
  were not a number either, and their conversion gave 2^63 bits with
  GCC 14. It throws `std::invalid_argument` now, as its documentation
  says (upgrade note 5).
- **simplex** (#235): `Simplex` and `Static_Event_Table` left such a
  count to `new[]` too, for which Clang, and GCC with items that have a
  destructor, ask the allocator for the largest `size_t`. They refuse it
  now (upgrade note 5).
- **noexcept** (#235): the functions of upgrade note 6 were `noexcept`
  when the implicit conversion of the result to `bool` did not throw, or
  the call alone in the searches of the containers and in the sorts, and
  they take the result as a condition: with a result whose conversion
  there throws, they ended the program instead of letting the exception
  reach the caller. The sorts of lists chose by it the way with nothing
  to undo. They look now at the conversion that they make, of what they
  compare. The functors that compare by default looked at the operator
  of the items, and not at the conversion of its result to the `bool`
  that they return: every heap, sort and set that compares with
  `Aleph::less` was in that case. `find_node()` and `find_arc()` were
  `noexcept` whatever the `==` of what a graph holds, and the two
  `Digraph_Iterator` whatever their filter. And `traverse()` negated
  what its operation returns, which runs the negation of the result
  where its specification looks at the conversion. With a comparison or
  a filter that returns a `bool`, GCC 14 and Clang 18 give the same code
  as before.
- **noexcept** (#235): the walk on the arcs into a node of a digraph,
  which `In_Iterator` is there, and the filter of the arcs that Kruskal
  paints give the filter of the caller an arc that has a name, and were
  `noexcept` by the call with one that has none: with a filter that
  tells the two apart, they ended the program. They look now at the call
  that they make. And `end()` of the `Digraph_Iterator` of `tpl_graph.H`
  was `noexcept` whatever its filter: it is now when the filter is
  (upgrade note 6).
- **heap** (#235): `heapsort()` and `faster_heapsort()` took the items,
  which they number from 1, through a pointer to one item before the
  array, which may not be formed. They take them now by their numbers,
  with no such pointer. Sorting 20000 items runs the same instructions
  with integers and floating point numbers, and from 6.2 % fewer to 0.5
  % more with strings. The two are `constexpr` now: in a constant
  expression, a pointer before the array is an error that Clang reports.

## Documentation

- **heap** (#235): `ArrayHeap` says now what it keeps when an operation
  of an item throws, its copy, its move or its exchange with another
  item: the guarantee is the basic one, as in the containers of the
  standard library. The heap has the count that it had or the one that
  the operation was giving it, and each entry holds an item as that
  operation left it; an item may be lost or moved from, and the items
  may be out of order. Its operations said already what they leave when
  the comparison throws.

## Known limits

- On `Treap`, `Treap_Rk` and `Rand_Tree`, the tree that the move of a
  `DynSetTree` builds for the set that it moves from makes its generator
  of random numbers, a block of `malloc()` of 2528 bytes, with any key.
  The `noexcept` of the move does not count it: without memory for it,
  the program ends. And the move of a `DynSetLinHash` or a
  `DynMapLinHash` builds a linear table, whose `DynArray` makes its
  directory with `malloc()`, and a default value that holds a key built
  by default. Both are left for 7.9.0, with a move of a tree that builds
  nothing, which will make the move of a set `noexcept` with every key
  again.
- The move constructor of a hash table copies its hash function, so that
  the table that it moves from keeps one that goes with its comparator.
  A pointer to a function, and a function object that fits in the buffer
  of a `std::function`, are copied without allocating; with one that
  does not fit, the move of a `LhashTable`, a `DynSetLhash`, a
  `DynSetHash`, a `DynMapHash` or an `ODhashTable` allocates once, in a
  constructor that is `noexcept`. Their move assignments copy nothing
  but a comparator that cannot be moved.
- MSVC moves the arguments of a constructor that a class inherits: with
  a comparator that can be copied and not moved, as one whose move
  constructor is deleted, it cannot build a `LhashTable`, a
  `LhashTableVtl`, a `DynLhashTable`, a `DynSetLhash` or a `DynMapHash`,
  however they are built, since they are built through the constructors
  that `LhashTable` and `LhashTableVtl` inherit. GCC, Clang and clang-cl
  build them.
- `Primes::next_prime()`, which the hash tables call with the count of
  buckets asked for, takes an `unsigned long`, of 32 bits on 64-bit
  Windows: a count of 2^32 buckets or more is cut there. And a count
  whose bytes fit in a `size_t` and that no machine has, as 2^59, still
  goes to it, which takes seconds, before `new[]` throws.
- The places where the result of a comparison is a condition inside a
  function that says `noexcept` are those that were found, and not a
  search of the library. The same holds for the tests of a range that a
  number that is not a number passes, as that of
  `Bloom_Filter::estimate()` was: other headers that take a probability
  or a density, as `random_graph.H`, `random_net.H` and
  `tpl_ca_stochastic_rules.H`, have them.
- `insertion_sort()` of an array loses an item when the comparison
  throws while it moves the items: the item that it took out is not put
  back.
- `Simplex::create_restriction()` loses the restriction that it
  allocated if its list has no memory for it.
- In `ArrayHeap`, with a comparison that may throw, a removed item that
  cannot be emptied without a throw stays in its entry, with what it
  holds, and so does the last item of the array with any comparison,
  until another item takes the entry or the array is destroyed.
- A move of the items of a `DynArrayHeap` that throws still leaves the
  heap out of order. When the key of `Fibonacci_Heap::decrease_key()`
  throws in its assignment, the node keeps what the assignment left, and
  may be out of order.
- In the interval tree, if the last assignment of a maximum throws, a
  move assignment that throws or the copy assignment of an endpoint that
  has no other, the maxima of some nodes may be left changed, though no
  node moves: the queries may then miss intervals, and the tree must be
  destroyed or built again.
- The iterators of the library keep their positions in a `long`, of 32
  bits on 64-bit Windows: on a container of 2^31 items or more, which
  `DynArray` admits, they would give a negative number of items. They
  will change together, in a later release.
- A splay tree builds a key by default in each search, insertion and
  removal, for the header node of its splay, inside `noexcept`: as
  `Splay_Tree` and `Splay_Tree_Rk` document, the default construction of
  the key must not throw.
