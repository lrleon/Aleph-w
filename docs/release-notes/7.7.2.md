# Aleph-w 7.7.2 release notes

Language: English | [Español](7.7.2.es.md)

Aleph-w 7.7.2 is a patch release that fixes two patterns of defects that
the Coverity Scan of the library reported, searched for in the whole of
it. Dozens of classes left a moved-from object that read or wrote
storage that it no longer had: they now leave it empty and usable.
Functions declared `noexcept` that run code of the user, as a comparison
or the copy of a key, or that throw when a precondition fails, ended the
program: the exception now reaches the caller, and the container stays
valid. On the way, the hash containers keep finding their keys after
`set_hash_fct()`, the iterators of `Avl_Tree_Rk` and `Rb_Tree_Rk`
compile, and `DynMapTree` searches without allocating, but over a splay
tree.

It is source compatible with 7.7.1, but for the changes listed in the
[Upgrade notes](#upgrade-notes): two copies that were wrong no longer
compile, some moves and searches are no longer `noexcept`, and
`set_hash_fct()` of a linear hash table that holds keys throws. As with
every release, recompile everything against the new headers
([`VERSIONING.md`](../../VERSIONING.md#abi-compatibility)).

## Upgrade notes

1. `find_package(Aleph 7 ...)` accepts 7.7.2.
2. Copying a `Hashlife_Engine` or a `Tile_Cache` no longer compiles.
   Their copies were wrong: that of an engine kept pointers into the
   nodes of the original and used them after the original freed them,
   and that of a cache kept iterators into the list of the original and
   wrote the same files.
3. A moved-from object of the classes that the [Fixes](#fixes) list
   under #201 is empty and usable: a move constructor leaves it empty,
   and a move assignment leaves in it either nothing or what the
   destination had, as each class documents. In 7.7.1, many of them read
   or wrote storage that they no longer had. A moved-from
   `Cuckoo_Filter` keeps the capacity of one bucket, and a moved-from
   `Quotient_Filter` or `Compact_Quotient_Filter` that of two slots.
4. The move constructors of `Cuckoo_Filter`, `Quotient_Filter`,
   `Compact_Quotient_Filter`, `SkipList`, `DynSkipList`, `Rand_Tree`,
   `Random_Set`, `In_Arcs_Index`, `Aho_Corasick`, `Suffix_Automaton` and
   `Naive_Suffix_Tree` allocate what they leave to the source, and are
   no longer `noexcept` (their cost is under
   [Performance](#performance)). A growing `std::vector` moves only what
   cannot throw, so it copies the nine of them that can be copied, all
   but `SkipList` and `Rand_Tree`. `Array` moves its items as it grows,
   and `DynList` and `DynArray` never relocate theirs.
5. The move assignment of `Rand_Tree` (`Gen_Rand_Tree`) exchanges the
   two trees: the source gets the nodes, the generator and the
   comparison of the destination. 7.7.1 freed the nodes of the
   destination, which the tree does not own, and left the source with no
   generator, so that an insertion into it crashed. A program that moves
   a `Rand_Tree` of its own frees those nodes itself; `DynSetTree` and
   `DynMapTree` free theirs as before.
6. These functions run code of the user inside `noexcept`, so that an
   exception of that code ended the program. They are now `noexcept`
   only when that code cannot throw, and its exception reaches the
   caller:
   - the operations of the binary search trees and of their ranked
     iterators that compare (`BinTree`, the AVL, red-black, treap and
     randomized trees, and their ranked versions), which leave the tree
     valid, with its keys, when the comparison throws; the splay trees
     keep their `noexcept`, and their comparison must not throw;
   - the constructors of the nodes of the trees from a key, and the
     default and move constructors of `Dnode<T>`;
   - `get_curr_ne()` of the zip iterators of `ah-zip.H`, which is no
     longer `noexcept` over a `DynArray`, whose reading may allocate,
     nor over items whose copy may throw, as `std::string`;
   - the moves and `swap()` of the classes that keep a comparison, an
     operation, a policy or a filter of the user.

   With `int` keys and the comparisons of the library, the trees and
   their nodes keep their `noexcept`.
7. `search()`, `has()` and `contains()` of the hash maps `DynMapHash`,
   `DynMapLinHash`, `MapOLhash` and `MapODhash`, and of a `DynMapTree`
   over a splay tree, are `noexcept` only when building a pair from the
   key and a `Data` built by default cannot throw: not with
   `std::string` keys, whose copy may throw `std::bad_alloc`, which
   ended the program. With keys and data such as `int` they keep it.
8. `set_hash_fct()` of the chained hash tables (`LhashTable` and the
   containers over it, as `DynSetHash` and `DynMapHash`) and of the open
   ones (`OLhashTable`, `ODhashTable` and their maps), and
   `set_second_hash_fct()` of `ODhashTable`, place the stored keys again
   with the new function, in O(n): 7.7.1 asked to call
   `resize(capacity())` or `rehash()` right after, which is no longer
   needed. If that fails, the table keeps its function and its keys.
   That of the linear hash tables (`LinearHashTable`, `DynSetLinHash`
   and `DynMapLinHash`), which cannot place them again, throws
   `std::domain_error` on a table that is not empty, where 7.7.1 took
   the function and then did not find the keys. None of them is
   `noexcept` any more.
9. `set_hash_fct()` of `DynMapHash` and `DynMapLinHash` also takes a
   hash function of the keys, `size_t (*)(const Key &)`, as their
   constructor does, and they keep searching by it. After a function of
   the pairs, which they took before, they still find their pairs, which
   `DynMapHash` missed: their searches then build a pair from the key
   and a `Data` built by default.
10. `Rand_Tree::split_pos()` throws `std::out_of_range` for a position
    past the end, before it changes anything, where it ended the
    program.
11. An iterator of `Treap_Rk`, `Avl_Tree_Rk` or `Rb_Tree_Rk` put at a
    position past the end is at the end: `has_curr()` is false, and
    `get_curr()` gives `nullptr`. 7.7.1 ended the program there, with
    `Treap_Rk`; the iterator of the other two did not compile.
12. `Simplex::put_restriction()` throws `std::logic_error` after
    `prepare_linear_program()`, which builds the tableau: 7.7.1 took the
    restriction, which the tableau did not have, and the solver read
    past it.
13. `Stationary_Detector` lets `std::bad_alloc` out of its callbacks,
    with its two buffers as they were, where it ended the program. Once
    its window is full, it allocates nothing.
14. New in the API: a `swap()` in the classes of #201 that had none, the
    default constructors of `Convex_Hull_Trick` and `RangeTree2D`,
    `SkipList::get_level()`, the `set_hash_fct()` of upgrade note 9,
    `inorder_position_of_node()` of `tpl_binNodeXt.H`, and the traits
    `nothrow_compare_v` and `nothrow_condition_v` of
    `tpl_binNodeUtils.H`, which tell whether a comparison can throw as
    the trees call it.

## Fixes

- **structures** (#201): a moved-from segment tree, sparse table,
  Cartesian tree, Fenwick tree, heap, B or B+ tree, persistent
  container, skip list, randomized tree, `Pointer_Table`,
  `Li_Chao_Tree`, `Convex_Hull_Trick` or `ModularCombinatorics` kept the
  sizes, counts, levels or roots of the storage that the move took: its
  checked accesses threw from `noexcept` functions, the unchecked ones
  read memory that it did not own, and the trees reported keys that they
  no longer had (upgrade note 3).
- **filters** (#201): the same in the cuckoo and quotient filters.
- **graph** (#201): the same in the LCA, the heavy-light and centroid
  decompositions, the tree DP, Mo on trees, `Floyd_All_Shortest_Paths`,
  `In_Arcs_Index`, `Hungarian_Assignment` and `Simplex`.
- **fft** (#201): the same in the FFT and NTT plans, convolvers and
  filters; `overlap_size()` of an empty overlap-add bank is 0.
- **strings** (#201): the same in `Aho_Corasick` and the suffix
  structures.
- **geometry** (#201): the same in `RangeTree2D` and `AABBTree`.
- **search** (#201): the same in `SearchSolutionCollector`.
- **ca** (#201): the same in the engines, lattices and observers of the
  cellular automata. A moved-from `Hashlife_Engine` read the nodes of
  the engine that took them, even after that engine freed them. Its
  copies, and those of `Tile_Cache`, no longer compile (upgrade note 2).
- **concurrency** (#201): a moved-from guard of `Synchronized` or
  `RwSynchronized` (`LockedPtr` and the others) still gave the value,
  with no lock held; it now gives none.
- **csv** (#201): a moved-from `CsvReader` reads nothing.
- **dynarray** (#201): `DynArray::swap()` exchanged the default values
  of the two arrays last, so that, when that swap threw, the arrays were
  left exchanged and their default values not. When it may throw, it
  goes first now, and then nothing else is exchanged. `DynArrayHeap` has
  a `swap()` that keeps that order.
- **trees** (#204): a comparison that threw in an operation of a tree
  ended the program (upgrade note 6).
- **trees** (#204): `del()` and `remove_pos()` of `DynSetTree`, and the
  removals of `DynMapTree`, lost the node that they took out, and kept
  counting it, when the copy of what they return threw. They free it
  now, and `size()` counts the keys that remain.
- **trees** (#204): the iterator of `Treap_Rk` had no current node after
  `reset_to_node()`, and a position given as a number that an `int`
  cannot hold, as 4294967298, lost its high bits and gave another node
  (that of the position 2); it is past the end now (upgrade note 11).
- **rand-tree** (#204): `split_pos()` past the end threw inside
  `noexcept` (upgrade note 10).
- **lists** (#204): `del_ne()` of the iterators of `Dlink` and `DynList`
  called the checked functions, which throw, inside `noexcept`; it calls
  the unchecked ones, as the other `_ne` functions do, and documents its
  precondition, a current item.
- **skiplist** (#204): `SkipList::remove()` never lowered the level of
  the list, since it compared the top level with `nullptr` instead of
  the sentinel, and read one slot past the top one. The level now goes
  down to that of the tallest node left.
- **simplex** (#204): a restriction put after the tableau existed made
  the solver read past it (upgrade note 12). A preparation that throws
  leaves the program taking restrictions, and the messages of the index
  errors print the ranges as [0, n), which wrapped around when n was 0.
- **ca** (#204): `Stationary_Detector` with a window of `SIZE_MAX`
  removed from an empty buffer at its first step and ended the program.
  An allocation that fails no longer ends it (upgrade note 13).
- **nodes** and **zip** (#204): the constructors of the nodes and of
  `Dnode`, and `get_curr_ne()` of the zip iterators, copied or moved
  keys and items inside `noexcept` (upgrade note 6).
- **hash** (#204): the linear hash tables ended the program when the
  copy of a key threw as they grew or shrank. A growth lets that
  exception reach the caller now, before it changes anything, and a
  shrinking, as in `empty()` and the destructor, keeps the empty list
  that it could not reset.
- **hash** (#204): a `resize()` of a chained hash table to fewer lists,
  or the growth of one well above its load factor, could grow it again
  in the middle, and a failed allocation there left keys out of the
  table and lost its old lists. It now allocates the final lists before
  it moves a key, and a `std::bad_alloc` leaves the table as it was.
  `set_hash_fct()` left the keys where the new function did not look for
  them (upgrade note 8).
- **maps** (#204): `DynMapHash` searched by the hash function of the
  keys given to its constructor even after `set_hash_fct()` (upgrade
  note 9); `MapOLhash` and `MapODhash` built the pair that they search
  for inside `noexcept` (upgrade note 7); and `DynMapTree` built a pair
  from the key and a `Data()` for each search, which allocated with a
  long `std::string` key or a `Data` that allocates. Over any tree but a
  splay one, it now compares the key with that of each pair, and
  allocates nothing.

## Performance

The figures of #204 compare with 7.7.1, with GCC 14 and Clang 18
(`-O2`), on a P core: the median of 7 to 9 rounds in each of four
alignments of the code, for the operations alone, without the building
of their data.

- **maps** (#204): a search of `DynMapTree` over a tree that is not a
  splay one builds no pair, and allocates nothing. With `int` keys it
  runs the instructions of 7.7.1 in the same cycles (0.1 % fewer with
  GCC, 0.7 % fewer with Clang). With keys that are not scalars, as
  `std::string`, it descends to a leaf with one comparison per level,
  which makes fewer comparisons. On hits in an AVL tree of 10^5
  `std::string` keys it runs 25 to 37 % fewer instructions; its cycles
  grow from 0.2 to 5.3 % with GCC 14, by the keys and the run, but 8 %
  with Clang 18 on keys of up to 7 characters and 24 to 27 % on keys of
  about 60: Clang compiles that descent without branches, so that each
  level waits for the comparison of the one above.
- **hash** (#204): a search of `DynMapLinHash` builds no pair either: 38
  to 41 % fewer instructions and 11 to 16 % fewer cycles, on 5 × 10^6
  searches of `int` keys in a map of 10^5. A search of `DynMapHash`,
  which checks when it misses whether the table has a function of the
  pairs, runs 1 to 3 % more instructions and 0.4 to 1.4 % more cycles,
  on hits and on misses.
- **trees** (#204): with `int` keys and `std::less`, the operations of
  the trees keep the object code of 7.7.1, but the insertion of
  `TdRbTree`, whose time does not change. The positions given as numbers
  to the iterator of `Treap_Rk` run 2.4 % fewer instructions with GCC 14
  and 1.4 % more with Clang 18, and their cycles change within the
  noise.
- **ca** (#204): a step of `Stationary_Detector` runs fewer
  instructions, 4 to 16 % with GCC 14 and 1 % with Clang 18. With GCC
  its cycles go from 1.9 % fewer with a window of 4 frames to 2.5 % more
  with the default of 16 and 5.4 % more with 48; with Clang, from 0.2 to
  1.4 % fewer.
- **moves** (#201): the moves that leave the source the storage that it
  needs, as a random generator, allocate it. The least of 15 move
  constructions of an object of 100 items takes 0.73 to 0.75 µs for
  `SkipList`, `DynSkipList`, `Rand_Tree` and `Random_Set`, which
  allocate a random generator for the source, 1.25 µs for
  `Compact_Quotient_Filter`, 0.79 µs for `Aho_Corasick`, 0.63 µs for
  `Suffix_Automaton`, 0.36 µs for `Naive_Suffix_Tree`, 0.15 µs for
  `Hashlife_Engine`, 0.13 µs for `Cuckoo_Filter`, and 21 to 40 ns for
  `Quotient_Filter`, `In_Arcs_Index` and `CsvReader` (GCC 14). Every
  other move of the classes of #201 is `noexcept` and takes 67 ns or
  less.
- **filters** (#201): with one bucket or two slots left to the source of
  a move, the operations of `Cuckoo_Filter`, `Quotient_Filter` and
  `Compact_Quotient_Filter` keep their object code, but for the line
  numbers in the messages of their errors. `Compact_Cuckoo_Filter`
  checks for a filter without buckets, which changes its times within
  the noise.

## Tests

- Each fix has tests that fail without it.
- `Tests/moved_from.H` is a typed test of the moved-from objects: it
  moves each object by construction and by assignment, and uses both
  objects as an ordinary user does. 87 types, the four sketches of 7.7.1
  among them, go through it in `Tests/moved_from_*_test.cc`.
  `policy-checks` runs `scripts/ci_moved_from_check.rb`, which fails a
  pull request that gives the pattern to a class.
- Pattern tests make the code of the user throw at each of its calls:
  `Tests/throwing_compare_test.cc` the comparison of every tree but the
  splay ones, and of `DynSetTree` and `DynMapTree` over them;
  `Tests/throwing_copy_test.cc` the copies and moves of keys and items;
  and `Tests/noexcept_contract_test.cc` the types of the user that the
  classes keep, in their moves and `swap()`.
  `Tests/hash_function_change_test.cc` checks that every hash container
  finds its keys after `set_hash_fct()`, empty and with keys.
- `Tests/hash_function_allocation_test.cc`,
  `Tests/ca_observer_allocation_test.cc` and
  `Tests/simplex_allocation_test.cc` make each allocation fail in turn.
