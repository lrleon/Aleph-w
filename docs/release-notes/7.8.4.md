# Aleph-w 7.8.4 release notes

Language: English | [Español](7.8.4.es.md)

Aleph-w 7.8.4 is a patch release. It fixes the removal of the two
quotient filters, which left a filter denying items that it held, or
looping for ever. And it makes the quotient filters, the lists of the
keys, values and items of a container and the optimal binary search
tree run fewer instructions, after the Coverity scan of 7.8.3, which
found no defect.

It is source compatible with 7.8.3. As with every release, recompile
everything against the new headers
([`VERSIONING.md`](../../VERSIONING.md#abi-compatibility)).

## Upgrade notes

1. `find_package(Aleph 7 ...)` accepts 7.8.4.
2. `Quotient_Filter` and `Compact_Quotient_Filter` keep every item that
   they hold through the removals of the others, as their headers
   promise. In every earlier version since 3.1.1, where they were added,
   a filter from which an item was removed could deny items that it
   held, refuse to remove them, or not return from a call.

## Fixes

- **filter** (#230): `remove()` of `Quotient_Filter` and of
  `Compact_Quotient_Filter` shifts the rest of the cluster one slot to
  the left, and marked as not shifted a run start that landed on a slot
  whose occupied bit was set. That bit says that the quotient of the
  slot has a run, which may be a later one, further to the right: the
  run that landed there was then taken for the start of a cluster, and
  the items after it were searched in the wrong runs. A filter of 16
  slots with 13 items denied one of them after two removals; and of 500
  runs of random insertions and removals against the exact set of the
  items, 361 gave a wrong answer and one did not end, in each of the
  two filters. The shift now follows the quotient of each run that it
  moves, the next occupied one after that of the run before it, and
  unshifts a run start only on the slot of its own quotient (upgrade
  note 2).

## Performance

Instructions counted by cachegrind at `-O2 -DNDEBUG`, from 7.8.3 to
7.8.4, with GCC 14 and with Clang 18.

- **filter** (#230): the accessors of the slots of the two quotient
  filters, all `noexcept`, went through checked accesses: `read_bit()`
  and `write_bit()` of `BitArray` in `Compact_Quotient_Filter`, the
  second through `DynArray::touch()`, and the `operator []` of `Array`
  in the reads of `Quotient_Filter`. Every constructor reserves the
  slots, and a slot is indexed below their count: they use
  `fast_read()`, `fast_write()` and the `operator ()` of `Array` now.
  On a filter of 4096 slots at 73 % of its capacity, `insert()` of a
  `Quotient_Filter` runs 11.3 % fewer instructions with GCC 14 and
  27.2 % fewer with Clang 18, `contains()` 8.0 % and 40.8 % fewer, and
  `remove()`, which the fix above makes do more, 13.5 % and 32.0 %
  fewer; in a `Compact_Quotient_Filter`, `insert()` runs 42.5 % and
  46.7 % fewer, `contains()` 22.4 % and 38.3 % fewer, and `remove()`
  39.7 % and 42.2 % fewer.
- **maps** (#230): `keys()` and `values()` of `DynMapTree`,
  `DynMapHashTable` and `MapOpenHash`, `to_DynList()` of an `Array`,
  `to_dynlist()`, `items()` and `keys()` of the lists and of the other
  containers of `GenericItems`, and `keys()` of `KeysMixin`, gave
  `maps()` a function that returned the item by value: a copy, which
  `maps()` then moved into the list. It returns a reference now, so
  that each item is copied once into the list and not moved. With keys
  or values that are strings of 40 characters, `keys()` and `values()`
  of maps of 2000 pairs run from 0.5 to 2.1 % fewer instructions;
  `values()` of a `DynMapTree` of items of 64 bytes, 7.8 % fewer with
  GCC 14 and the same with Clang 18.
- **opBinTree** (#230): `build_optimal_tree()` read the matrices of
  costs and of roots, and the prefix sums, with the `operator []` of
  `DynArray`, which tests whether the entry is there and may allocate
  it. Its recurrence reads only entries of shorter intervals, which it
  wrote before: it reads them with `access()` now. With 300 keys it
  runs 37.4 % fewer instructions with GCC 14 and 59.5 % fewer with
  Clang 18.

## Tests

- `Tests/quotient_filter_removal_test.cc`, new, on both filters: every
  count of items in filters of 2 to 128 slots, removed one by one, with
  every other item asked for after each removal; random insertions and
  removals against the exact set of the items; and a filter filled with
  as many items as it has slots, searched, emptied and cleared in each
  of the states that it may come to be in, built, copied, assigned,
  moved to, moved from and swapped. Its 6 cases fail on 7.8.3, and each
  of ten mutations of the fix fails them or makes them loop.
- `Tests/item_copies_test.cc` counts the moves too, in the twelve kinds
  of maps with the counted item as their value and as their key, and in
  the lists of the items of a container: 23 of its 27 cases fail on
  7.8.3.
- `Tests/opbintree_test.cc`, new, since `opBinTree.H` had no test: the
  cost of the tree against the least one that trying every root gives,
  for up to 40 keys with random weights, ties and zeros, and its keys
  in order.
- The 29 test programs that use what changes run under ASan and UBSan
  with GCC 14, 2299 tests, with no report.
- The platform workflow passes: on Windows with MSVC, which runs 13 539
  tests, and with clang-cl, and on macOS and on ARM64 Linux.

## Known limits

- On `Treap`, `Treap_Rk` and `Rand_Tree`, the tree that the move of a
  `DynSetTree` builds for the set that it moves from makes its generator
  of random numbers, a block of `malloc()` of 2528 bytes, with any key.
  The `noexcept` of the move does not count it: without memory for it,
  the program ends. And the move of a `DynSetLinHash` or a
  `DynMapLinHash` builds a linear table, whose `DynArray` makes its
  directory with `malloc()`, and a default value that holds a key built
  by default. Both are left for 7.9.0, with a move of a tree that builds
  nothing, which will make the move of a set `noexcept` with every key
  again.
- The move constructor of a hash table copies its hash function, so
  that the table that it moves from keeps one that goes with its
  comparator. A pointer to a function, and a function object that fits
  in the buffer of a `std::function`, are copied without allocating;
  with one that does not fit, the move of a `LhashTable`, a
  `DynSetLhash`, a `DynSetHash`, a `DynMapHash` or an `ODhashTable`
  allocates once, in a constructor that is `noexcept`. Their move
  assignments copy nothing but a comparator that cannot be moved.
- MSVC moves the arguments of a constructor that a class inherits: with
  a comparator that can be copied and not moved, as one whose move
  constructor is deleted, it cannot build a `LhashTable`, a
  `LhashTableVtl`, a `DynLhashTable`, a `DynSetLhash` or a `DynMapHash`,
  however they are built, since they are built through the constructors
  that `LhashTable` and `LhashTableVtl` inherit. GCC, Clang and clang-cl
  build them.
- `Static_Event_Table` and `Simplex` leave to `new[]` a count of the
  caller whose bytes may not fit in a `size_t`, as `MemArray` did, and
  the hash tables do the same with their buckets, after they round
  their length up to a prime.
- In `ArrayHeap`, with a comparison that cannot throw, a move
  assignment of an item that throws in `remove()` leaves the entry of
  the removed item as that assignment left it. With a comparison that
  may throw, a removed item that cannot be emptied without a throw
  stays in its entry, with what it holds, and so does the last item of
  the array with any comparison, until another item takes the entry or
  the array is destroyed.
- A move of the items of a `DynArrayHeap` that throws still leaves the
  heap out of order. When the key of `Fibonacci_Heap::decrease_key()`
  throws in its assignment, the node keeps what the assignment left,
  and may be out of order.
- In the interval tree, if the last assignment of a maximum throws, a
  move assignment that throws or the copy assignment of an endpoint
  that has no other, the maxima of some nodes may be left changed,
  though no node moves: the queries may then miss intervals, and the
  tree must be destroyed or built again.
- `heapsort()` and `faster_heapsort()` still form a pointer to one item
  before the array when they sort two items or more.
- Some `noexcept` of the library, in the interval tree and in the graphs
  among others, look at the implicit conversion of the result of a
  comparison to `bool`, where the code makes the one of a condition: for
  a result whose two conversions differ, they may promise what the call
  does not keep. The searches of the containers, as `find_ptr()`, and
  the sorts of `tpl_sort_utils.H` look at the call alone, whatever the
  conversion does.
- The iterators of the library keep their positions in a `long`, of 32
  bits on 64-bit Windows: on a container of 2^31 items or more, which
  `DynArray` admits, they would give a negative number of items. They
  will change together, in a later release.
- `Dft_Map_Op::operator()` and `Dft_Fold_Op::operator()`, the default
  operations of `maps()` and `foldl()`, are `noexcept` and build an
  item: `maps<T>()` of a container made of the mixins of
  `ah-dry-mixin.H`, called with no operation, ends the program if the
  copy of an item throws. And `maps<R>()` with no operation, of such a
  container, converts each item to `R`, back to its own type and to `R`
  again, which does not compile for some types and gives another value
  for some items.
- A full `Compact_Quotient_Filter` throws `std::overflow_error` on the
  insertion of an item whose fingerprint it holds already, which
  `Quotient_Filter` takes as the duplicate that it is. `merge()` of
  `Compact_Quotient_Filter` returns without the items of the other
  filter that do not fit, so that the filter then denies items that it
  was given; that of `Quotient_Filter` throws there with some of them
  in, and for an item that the full filter holds too.
- `from_capacity()` and `estimate()` of the two quotient filters give a
  filter with fewer slots than items when the count of items and the
  rate of false positives ask together for more than the 64 bits of the
  hash, and another rate for one that asks for more than 255 remainder
  bits.
- A splay tree builds a key by default in each search, insertion and
  removal, for the header node of its splay, inside `noexcept`: as
  `Splay_Tree` and `Splay_Tree_Rk` document, the default construction
  of the key must not throw.
